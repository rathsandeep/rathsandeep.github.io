  1. Optimal Staffing and Treatment for Collaborative Care of Diabetes and Depression
    Sandeep Rath, Jayashankar M Swaminathan, and Charles A Coleman Jr
    Production and Operations Management, 2026
    Abs SSRN POM

    About 27 percent of patients with diabetes also have depression. We develop a Markov dynamic programming model that jointly sets care manager staffing and treatment policies for Collaborative Care programs, balancing patients’ health outcomes against the clinic’s financial sustainability, and calibrate it with data from a large academic medical center.

  2. Multilocation, Dynamic Staff Planning for a Healthcare System: Methodology and Application
    Sandeep Rath, Kumar Rajaram, Mark E Hudson, and Aman Mahajan
    Operations Research, 2026
    Abs SSRN OR

    We develop a data-driven, multistage robust optimization model that assigns anesthesiologists across the hospitals of a large health system. An on-call pool absorbs demand uncertainty, and the model respects individual location constraints and fairness in on-call duty. The model was implemented across 11 hospitals at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center.

  3. Frontiers in Operations: Does Physician’s Choice of When to Perform EHR Tasks Influence Total EHR Workload?
    Umit Celik, Sandeep Rath, Saravanan Kesavan, and Bradley R. Staats
    Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, 2024
    Abs SSRN MSOM

    Using about 150,000 appointments from 74 physicians, we study how the timing of EHR work affects physician workload. EHR work done before an appointment reduces both total EHR time and after-hours work, while work done after an appointment reduces after-hours work but increases total EHR time.

  4. Data-Driven Surgical Tray Optimization to Improve Operating Room Efficiency
    Vinayak Deshpande, Nishanth Mundru, Sandeep Rath, Martyn Knowles , and 2 more authors
    Operations Research, 2024
    Abs OR

    Using instrument-level usage data from a large hospital, we build an optimization model that reconfigures surgical instrument trays to cut the cost of sterilizing, assembling, and repurchasing unused instruments. The method scales to thousands of surgeries and instruments. The recommended trays were implemented at UNC Rex Hospital. This work was a finalist for the INFORMS Innovative Applications in Analytics Award (2022) and received the Bullard Faculty Research Impact Award (2024).

  5. Staff Planning for Hospitals with Implicit Cost Estimation and Stochastic Optimization
    Sandeep Rath, and Kumar Rajaram
    Production and Operations Management, 2022
    Abs SSRN POM

    We study monthly and daily anesthesiologist staffing decisions, including whom to place on call and how many on-call staff to call in. We estimate the implicit costs of these decisions from the hospital’s past choices and embed them in a two-stage stochastic integer program, using data from UCLA Ronald Reagan Medical Center.

  6. Integrated Anesthesiologist and Room Scheduling for Surgeries: Methodology and Application
    Sandeep Rath, Kumar Rajaram, and Aman Mahajan
    Operations Research, 2017
    Abs OR

    We develop a two-stage stochastic optimization model that jointly assigns anesthesiologists and operating rooms to surgeries with uncertain durations and sequences the surgeries. As a decision support system, it raised daily utilization of anesthesiologists by 3.5 percent and of operating rooms by 3.8 percent. The model was implemented at UCLA Ronald Reagan Medical Center.

  7. Planning for HIV Screening, Testing, and Care at the Veterans Health Administration
    Sarang Deo, Kumar Rajaram, Sandeep Rath, Uday S Karmarkar , and 1 more author
    Operations Research, 2015
    Abs SSRN OR

    We determine how many patients to screen for HIV in each period and how to staff each part of the care system to maximize quality-adjusted life-years gained. Applied to the Greater Los Angeles VA, the model shows that routine screening of all patients may not be feasible within budget and identifies better policies than screening only high-risk patients.

working papers

  1. Right Basket, Right Coupon: Basket-Aware Promotion Optimization in Online Retail
    B. Li, S. Rath, Y. Xu, and A. Capponi
    Submitted to Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, 2026
    Abs SSRN

    We learn complement relationships from purchase data at a large US online grocery platform and estimate coupon spillovers from a 69-day experiment using double machine learning. A basket-aware coupon allocation policy built on these estimates raises the model-implied uplift in net GMV by 4.29 percent over an item-level policy with the same budget.

  2. Reducing Appointment Delays: The Impact of Standardized EHR Usage on Physician Timeliness
    U. Celik, S. Rath, and B. Staats
    Working Paper, 2025
    Abs SSRN HTML

    Using over 120,000 appointments from 57 physicians, we estimate the effect of standardized EHR documentation on appointment delays using double machine learning. Greater use of documentation templates reduces same-day delays but lengthens clinical notes and increases in-room time at the patient’s next visit.
