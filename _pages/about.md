I am an Assistant Professor of Operations Management at the Indian School of Business (ISB). Before joining ISB in 2025, I was on the faculty of UNC Kenan-Flagler Business School (2016–2025). I received my PhD in Management from UCLA Anderson.

I use optimization and causal inference to improve healthcare operations. With integer, stochastic, robust, and dynamic programming, I build models from electronic health record (EHR) data to plan clinical staff, operating rooms, and medical inventory; several have been implemented at US health systems. With causal inference methods such as instrumental variables and double machine learning, I study how digitization affects physician workload and patient delays. My work is published in *Operations Research*, *Production and Operations Management*, and *Manufacturing & Service Operations Management*.

My current research examines how digitization and AI are changing hospital operations in India, and how AI tools can improve hospital administration. I also apply these methods to other service settings, such as online retail.

At ISB, I teach operations management and service operations. I also advise startups, hospitals, healthcare accreditation bodies, and government agencies on health AI and digitization.
