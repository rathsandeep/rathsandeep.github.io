### Indian School of Business (2025–present)

- **Process Excellence in Healthcare Organizations** (Advanced Management Program in Healthcare), 2026. Core operations management course for healthcare executives.
- **Operations Management** (PGP core), 2025, 2026. Core operations management course in the PGP, ISB's MBA program.
- **Managing Services in the Digital World** (PGP elective), 2025–26. PGP (MBA) elective on service operations management.
- **Managing Service Organizations** (Advanced Management Program in Operations and Supply Chain), 2025. Service operations management course for operations and supply chain executives.

### UNC Kenan-Flagler Business School (2017–2025)

- **BUSI 532H: Healthcare and Service Operations Management** (undergraduate elective). Elective on healthcare and service operations management.
- **BUSI 403: Operations Management** (undergraduate core). Core operations management course for undergraduate business students.
