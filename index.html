Sandeep Rath

Indian School of Business

Hyderabad, India

sandeep_rath@isb.edu

I am an Assistant Professor of Operations Management at the Indian School of Business (ISB). Before joining ISB in 2025, I was on the faculty of the Kenan-Flagler Business School at UNC Chapel Hill (2016–2025). I received my PhD in Management from UCLA Anderson.

My research uses optimization and causal inference to improve hospital operations. Using integer, stochastic, robust, and dynamic programming, I build models estimated from electronic health record (EHR) data to plan clinical staff, operating rooms, and medical inventory under uncertain demand; several have been implemented at US health systems. Using causal inference methods such as instrumental variables and double machine learning, I study how digitization has changed physicians’ work, including how EHR use affects their workload and patient delays.

My papers are published in Operations Research, Production and Operations Management, and Manufacturing & Service Operations Management. My work received the Bullard Faculty Research Impact Award (UNC Kenan-Flagler, 2024) and was a finalist for the INFORMS Innovative Applications in Analytics Award (2022).

At ISB, I teach the core Operations Management course and an elective on Managing Services in a Digital World, along with executive courses in healthcare and service operations.

My current interests are in how digitization and AI are changing hospital operations in India, and how AI tools can improve the administrative functions of a hospital. I am also interested in applying causal inference and optimization methods to other service delivery settings, such as online retail.

Beyond research, I advise startups, hospitals, healthcare accreditation bodies, and government agencies on health AI and digitization. I welcome conversations with hospitals and companies interested in data-driven research partnerships.

recent publications

  1. Multilocation, Dynamic Staff Planning for a Healthcare System: Methodology and Application
    Sandeep Rath ,  Kumar Rajaram ,  Mark E Hudson ,  and  Aman Mahajan
    Operations Research, 2026
    Implemented across 11 hospitals at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center.
    Abs SSRN Operations Research

    We develop a data-driven, multistage robust optimization model that assigns anesthesiologists across the hospitals of a large health system. An on-call pool absorbs demand uncertainty, and the model respects individual location constraints and fairness in on-call duty.

  2. Frontiers in Operations: Does Physician’s Choice of When to Perform EHR Tasks Influence Total EHR Workload?
    Umit Celik ,  Sandeep Rath ,  Saravanan Kesavan ,  and  Bradley R. Staats
    Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, 2024
    Abs SSRN Manufacturing & Service Operations Management

    Using about 150,000 appointments from 74 physicians, we study how the timing of EHR work affects physician workload. EHR work done before an appointment reduces both total EHR time and after-hours work, while work done after an appointment reduces after-hours work but increases total EHR time.

  3. Data-Driven Surgical Tray Optimization to Improve Operating Room Efficiency
    Vinayak Deshpande ,  Nishanth Mundru ,  Sandeep Rath ,  Martyn Knowles , and 2 more authors
    Operations Research, 2024
    Implemented at UNC Rex Hospital. Finalist, INFORMS Innovative Applications in Analytics Award (2022). Bullard Faculty Research Impact Award (2024).
    Abs Operations Research

    Using instrument-level usage data from a large hospital, we build an optimization model that reconfigures surgical instrument trays to cut the cost of sterilizing, assembling, and repurchasing unused instruments. The method scales to thousands of surgeries and instruments.

  4. Integrated Anesthesiologist and Room Scheduling for Surgeries: Methodology and Application
    Sandeep Rath ,  Kumar Rajaram ,  and  Aman Mahajan
    Operations Research, 2017
    Implemented at UCLA Ronald Reagan Medical Center.
    Abs Operations Research

    We develop a two-stage stochastic optimization model that jointly assigns anesthesiologists and operating rooms to surgeries with uncertain durations and sequences the surgeries. As a decision support system, it raised daily utilization of anesthesiologists by 3.5 percent and of operating rooms by 3.8 percent.

working papers

  1. Right Basket, Right Coupon: Basket-Aware Promotion Optimization in Online Retail
    B. Li ,  S. Rath ,  Y. Xu ,  and  A. Capponi
    Submitted to Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, 2026
    Abs SSRN

    We learn complement relationships from purchase data at a large US online grocery platform and estimate coupon spillovers from a 69-day experiment using double machine learning. A basket-aware coupon allocation policy built on these estimates raises the model-implied uplift in net GMV by 4.29 percent over an item-level policy with the same budget.

  2. Reducing Appointment Delays: The Impact of Standardized EHR Usage on Physician Timeliness
    U. Celik ,  S. Rath ,  and  B. Staats
    Working Paper, 2025
    Abs SSRN HTML

    Using over 120,000 appointments from 57 physicians, we estimate the effect of standardized EHR documentation on appointment delays using double machine learning. Greater use of documentation templates reduces same-day delays but lengthens clinical notes and increases in-room time at the patient’s next visit.
